The problem

Talent and opportunity never meet

Too many students never learn about apprenticeships that suit them, while employers struggle to reach qualified candidates. Discovery is scattered across job boards, college noticeboards, and word of mouth (so the right opportunity rarely reaches the right student at the right time).

The fix

UCAS – a single, verified marketplace

We connect students directly with apprenticeship employers, making opportunities visible to the right audience without the noise. We focus on the gap between awareness and action, not just listing jobs, so candidates apply with confidence and employers reach who matters.

What UCAS does

Our platform focuses on key features that simplify discovery, matching, and application processes for both students and employers.

  1. 01

    Opportunity Discovery

    Students browse verified apprenticeship openings matched to their interests and location, with clear entry requirements and career pathways shown upfront. No guesswork, real requirements, and local filtering make the search focused and useful.

  2. 02

    Employer Matching

    Apprenticeship employers post roles once and reach students actively searching, with filters for qualifications, sectors, and regions to reduce time-to-hire. Targeted reach and faster hiring at lower cost than fragmented job boards.

  3. 03

    Application Tracking

    Students apply in a few clicks using a single profile, then track application status and receive feedback without juggling multiple portals. Fewer emails, real-time updates, and one profile carry them through every application.

  4. 04

    Marketplace Analytics

    Our dashboard shows employers application rates, student engagement, and regional demand so they can optimise listings and timing. Data‑driven decisions, spot trends early, and improve response quality over time.

Frequently asked questions

How do students find apprenticeships on UCAS?

They create a simple profile and browse verified listings filtered by location, sector, and entry requirements, then apply with one click.

What does it cost employers to post an apprenticeship?

Basic listings are free; promoted slots and analytics dashboards are available for a monthly fee.

Are apprenticeship opportunities verified?

Yes, every listing is reviewed to confirm it is a genuine, currently open apprenticeship role.

Can students apply to multiple roles at once?

Yes, students can apply to any number of matching roles and track each application separately.

How long does it take to hire through UCAS?

Most employers report first qualified applications within 7 days of posting.

Is UCAS only for school leavers?

No, any student aged 16+ seeking an apprenticeship can join, including college and university candidates.

What sectors are most represented?

Currently engineering, healthcare, digital, and construction, with new sectors added monthly.

How does UCAS make money?

Through optional promoted listings and premium employer subscriptions; core access for students remains free.
